02 · Why Filipino audiences matter

A large, internationally dispersed audience.

The Philippine Department of Foreign Affairs recorded about 10.8 million overseas Filipinos for July to December 2024. About 2.1 million were in the Middle East, about 4.4 million in the United States and about 974,000 in Canada. The audience is therefore spread across markets that differ in language, legal status, media habits and household needs, and a brand that wants to reach Filipinos usually has to think about more than one of them.

For categories such as remittance, financial services, telecoms, retail and consumer goods, Filipino communities are often a core audience rather than a niche. The commercial question is rarely whether the audience exists; it is which segment, in which market, through which channel, and how to measure it.

Source: Philippine DFA report to the Senate Committee on Foreign Relations (27 August 2025), as reported by GMA News. Official estimates of overseas Filipinos are not the same as campaign reach.

05 · Audience strategy

Combining signals instead of selecting “Filipino”.

Advertising platforms do not offer one reliable setting that finds every Filipino consumer. Strategy therefore combines geography, language, media consumption, context, search behaviour, video behaviour, content affinities, community interests, first-party or client data where available and permitted, and campaign learnings, and tests which combination performs for the objective. Adventis does not promise targeting that a platform does not support.

06 · Paid media

Google, YouTube, Meta and programmatic.

Google Ads across Search, YouTube and Display, using location, language, search intent, custom segments, placements and permitted first-party data.

YouTube

Video campaigns for awareness, consideration and traffic, judged by view rate and cost per view or click-through rate and cost per click depending on the objective.

Meta

Facebook and Instagram campaigns; TikTok where appropriate to the brief.

08 · Celebrity partnerships

Opportunities with Filipino celebrities and personalities.

Celebrity work differs from influencer marketing: it typically aims for credibility, scale or a high-visibility moment. Adventis can help brands identify and develop opportunities with relevant Filipino celebrities and personalities: talent sourcing, brand fit, ambassador and endorsement concepts, event and campaign appearances, digital content, social collaborations and entertainment partnerships.

Adventis does not represent talent and holds no exclusive celebrity relationships. We work on the brand's behalf; availability, fees and terms are set by the talent and their representatives.

10 · Audience intelligence

Research → Audience Strategy → Activation → Campaign Data → Learning → Optimisation.

Adventis develops knowledge of Filipino audiences from campaign learnings, audience and market research, media, search and video behaviour, geographic patterns, platform performance, content engagement, cultural context, community activity and, where permitted, client campaign data. 12 · Campaign methodology

How we work.

  1. Brief and objective. Market, audience, objective, budget, timing and required services.
  2. Audience and market scoping. Which Filipino segments in which markets, with sourced sizing and an honest view of what is proven and what is a test.
  3. Channel and creative plan. Paid media, creators, celebrities, events and community routes, with language and cultural adaptation.
  4. Launch with a test design. Test and control groups where budget allows, and campaign naming that makes cohorts identifiable.
  5. Measure against the right metric. Reach for awareness, view rate for video, click-through rate and conversions for search, first transactions for acquisition.
  6. Learn and optimise. Feed confirmed learnings into the next round of strategy and into audience intelligence.
